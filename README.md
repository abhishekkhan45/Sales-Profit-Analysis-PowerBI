Sales Performance & Profitability Dashboard – Power BI

📊 Project Overview

This project is an interactive Power BI Sales Performance & Profitability Dashboard built to analyze sales, profit, discounts, units sold, products, countries, segments, and time-based performance.

The dashboard converts raw sales data into interactive KPIs and visual insights that can help users understand overall business performance and identify important sales and profitability trends.

🎯 Objectives

Monitor total sales, total profit, and units sold.

Analyze sales and profit across countries and customer segments.

Identify product-level sales and profitability patterns.

Analyze discount performance.

Track sales trends by year, quarter, and month.

Provide interactive filtering for easier business analysis.

🔍 Key Analysis Areas

Sales Analysis

Analyze how total sales change across products, countries, segments, and time periods.

Profitability Analysis

Compare profit across products, countries, and customer segments to identify profitable and less-profitable areas.

Discount Analysis

Analyze the relationship between discount bands, sales, and profitability.

Product Analysis

Compare products based on sales, units sold, manufacturing price, and profit.

Time Analysis

Use year, quarter, and month-level analysis to understand sales trends over time.
